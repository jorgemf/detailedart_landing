## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Colors
- Primary: #8a4baf (Purple)
- Secondary: #7a3f9d (Purple)
- Accent: #8a4baf (Purple)
- Text: #1f2937 (Dark Gray)
- Background: #ffffff (White)

### Typography
- Headings: 'Montserrat', sans-serif
- Body: 'Open Sans', sans-serif
- Font Sizes:
  - H1: 2.5rem
  - H2: 2rem
  - H3: 1.5rem
  - Body: 1rem

### Responsive Breakpoints
- Mobile: < 768px
- Tablet: 768px - 1024px
- Desktop: > 1024px

## 📱 Responsive Design
- Mobile-first approach
- Fluid typography
- Flexible grid system
- Optimized images for different screen sizes
